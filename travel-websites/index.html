FAQs

Common questions

Can you build a website for a new travel business?

Yes. If you’re starting out, we can plan a site that presents your first packages professionally and grows with you.

Can customers submit booking requests online?

Yes. Every package includes a booking enquiry form so customers can request a booking. A full booking workflow depends on your agreed scope.

Can I receive booking alerts through email or WhatsApp?

Email alerts are part of the starting packages. WhatsApp alerts are included from the Pro package; messaging provider fees may apply.

Can online payments be integrated?

Payment gateway integration is possible depending on your requirements and the gateway you choose. It’s confirmed in your project scope, and gateway charges are separate.

Can I manage packages and availability?

Package management and availability management can be included where agreed in your project scope. Tell me what you need and I’ll confirm what’s possible.

Are third-party service charges included?

No. Payment gateway fees, messaging provider fees, paid plugins, hosting and similar costs may be separate depending on the final scope.

Can I upgrade the website later?

Yes. You can start with a smaller package and add features later, subject to a new scope and quotation.

How is the final project price determined?

By the features and integrations you need. The listed prices are starting points, and the final quotation is confirmed in writing before work begins.

Ready to discuss your travel website?

Tell me about your business and what you need. I’ll reply personally to discuss scope and next steps.
